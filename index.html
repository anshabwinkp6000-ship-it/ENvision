BRAMAYUGAM

“The Age Of Madness”

— Film

A House That Devours Its Guests

In 17th century Malabar, Thevan, a Paanan folk singer, escapes slave traders and stumbles into a decaying mana deep inside the forest. There he meets Kodumon Potti, an old Brahmin lord, and his silent cook. What begins as shelter turns into a chess game of power, hunger and sorcery from which no man walks out unchanged.

Shot entirely in black and white, Bramayugam turns feudal dread into folk horror — a chamber piece about the appetite of power itself.

— Music

The Sound of Darkness

Christo Xavier's score leans on ritual percussion, chants and long stretches of silence. Folk forms of Malabar are used not as ornament but as warning — the music tells you the house is listening.

— Recognition

Awards & Critical Acclaim

Bramayugam was received as one of the boldest Malayalam films of 2024 — praised for Mammootty's performance, Shehnad Jalal's monochrome cinematography and its refusal of easy scares.

Kerala State Film Awards

  • Best Actor — Mammootty (nomination consideration)
  • Best Cinematography — Shehnad Jalal
  • Best Art Direction — Jothish Shankar
  • Best Sound Design — Jayadevan Chakkadath

Critics & Industry Honours

  • Critics' Choice recognition for Best Malayalam Film
  • SIIMA nominations across acting and technical crafts
  • Widely listed among the best Indian films of 2024
  • International festival screenings of Indian horror cinema
